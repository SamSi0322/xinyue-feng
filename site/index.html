An Ace Attorney platformer

04 · Game AI · 2026

Tic-Tac-Toe vs. AI

Three opponents, one unbeatable

A browser game that shows how the computer thinks. Easy plays loosely, Medium is the rule-based AI from the original class assignment, and Hard is a minimax search that cannot lose. Switch on “Show AI thinking” to see its score for every square.

  • JavaScript
  • Minimax
  • Alpha–beta pruning
  • Accessibility

About

“Player one”

I’m Xinyue; most people call me Lily. I study Computer Science at Kean University, and I’m most interested in human–computer interaction: the point where software meets the person using it.

A lot of my projects start with something I love. Ace Attorney turned into a Java platformer; Harry Potter turned into a Terraria mod. Outside class I’ve interned in computer-vision data processing and at a game technology company, contributed to two published papers on medical image classification, and helped ship a prototype in a 48-hour game jam.
