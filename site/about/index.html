In short

The Java platformer I designed, drew and coded on my own grew out of a game I loved, Ace Attorney. Class work grew into a recipe app that turns what’s in your fridge into three recipes with photos, and a tic-tac-toe game with a rule-based AI opponent. In summer 2026 I built Wizarding World, a Harry Potter adventure for Terraria, with AI coding and image tools.

This fall I’ve been leading two bigger projects with AI tools: Yuzu Onsen Capybaras, a cozy pixel-art life sim you can play in the browser, and Otherworlds, a Terraria mod that brings in 38 bosses from five other games.

Much of my work has been in teams: a 48-hour game jam and internships in computer vision, game technology and sports-event operations.
