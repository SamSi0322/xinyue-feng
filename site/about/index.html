In short

Most of my projects begin with something I already love. A courtroom game I was playing became a Java platformer; the Harry Potter books became a Terraria mod with twelve boss fights. Class assignments, too, turned into things I wanted to finish properly: a recipe app that generates photos of its dishes and a tic-tac-toe AI that shows its reasoning.

I like working in teams. A 48-hour game jam, two research collaborations and two internships have all taught me how much good software depends on communicating clearly.
