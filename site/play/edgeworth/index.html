How to play

  • Winston Payne walks back and forth. Land on his head to knock him out.

  • Manfred von Karma can't be stomped. Jump over him.

  • Wendy Oldbag rises out of pipes and floats away. Don't touch her.

  • Phoenix blocks are worth 10 points. Hit them from below.

  • Detective Gumshoe knows where an invisible block is hidden.

Three lives. Falling off the screen or touching an enemy costs one, and puts you back at the start of the scene.

About this edition

Xinyue (Lily) Feng made the game in Java Swing for CPS 2231 at Wenzhou-Kean University (April–May 2024). She laid the levels out on a grid in Excel and made the sprites in Aseprite, based on characters from the Ace Attorney games.

This web edition ports her game logic from Java to JavaScript and the HTML canvas, keeping the movement speeds, jump timing, collision rules and all six levels unchanged. It also finishes two things she wrote about in her project report: the forest background is now animated, and sound effects play once, on cue.

Also fixed: after a death, a level now resets cleanly (the original duplicated defeated enemies), and the falling and "knocked out" sprites she drew are shown in play.
